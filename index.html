Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Data Collection

SUBKING stores all subscription data locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any external server.

AI Diagnosis

When you use the AI diagnosis feature, anonymized subscription data (service names, prices, categories) is sent to our secure API for analysis. No personally identifiable information is included.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Favicon API to display service logos. Only the service domain name is sent to retrieve the logo image. No user data is shared.

In-App Purchases

Pro subscriptions are processed through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment information.

Data Deletion

You can delete all data at any time from Settings > Delete all data. Uninstalling the app also removes all local data.
